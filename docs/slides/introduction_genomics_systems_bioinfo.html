Genomics, Systems Biology and Bioinformatics

Unified framework for multi-omics teaching

One framework, two pathways

  • Reuse concepts and worked examples across both harmonized MSc outlines.
  • Select depth and exercises to match each pathway’s learning objectives.
  • Keep study design, computation and biological interpretation connected.

Use the teaching materials

  1. Read the module and identify the measurement and assumptions.
  2. Run the practical and predict the effect of one change.
  3. Attempt exercises before opening suggested answers.
  4. Explain what the result supports and what needs further evidence.

Worked thread through the course

  • Plan an animal-level diet study and matched assays.
  • Verify sample identity and assay-specific quality.
  • Analyse RNA counts and expression contrasts.
  • Examine pathways and cross-layer relationships.
  • Compare predictive strategies with training-only transformations.
  • Write an interpretation or proposal at the level the evidence supports.
